Why this project?

PC gaming got overwhelming.

The best results come from external programs and mods, each with its own installer, its own folders and its own rules: many paths to remember, create and manage, spread over half a dozen launchers.

Hybrid Launcher brings some of the main mods and the usual launcher features together, and offers them as one alternative.

Not a beginner-first app. It is meant for people with some basic experience. Get familiar with the mods it handles, read their wikis, and understand how they affect your game and your game folder: you will get much more out of it, and you will know what is going on when something does not work.
Features

A launcher and a mod tool in one program.

Every picture below is the real app, on real games.

One library. Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Xbox / Game Pass, Ubisoft Connect, EA and Rockstar games in one list with any exe or folder you add, each with its cover, play time and a Play button.

DLSS → FSR 4

OptiScaler. Install the Stable or Nightly build, a community fork or a build of your own. When a newer build is out the game page says Update available, and you can get a notification for it. The settings you change most have their own panel, profiles keep different configurations, and every version you installed is saved so you can go back to it.

Mods tab. Every mod in the game folder, what loads it and whether it works, with a warning and a fix when a game update removed or replaced one. ReShade (standard or add-on build), RenoDX, Luma, DXVK and VKD3D-Proton install from here.

SDRHDR

Smart HDR. Windows HDR turns on when a game starts and off when it closes.

DLSS 5 on Radeon

DLSS 5 neural rendering (experimental). On Radeon RX 9000, mochizuki0323's DLSSNR-AMD goes in as an OptiScaler plugin, or through ReShade in any 64-bit game without OptiScaler. On RTX, janblade's F5 Multipass build. Hybrid Launcher says what your card can run and sets it up step by step; your own copy of NVIDIA's model file is used, nothing from NVIDIA is downloaded.

Achievements from every store. Steam, GOG, Xbox and Game Pass, and local files on your PC. Search them, see how rare each one is, and keep a history of every unlock with a screenshot of the moment, exportable as CSV or JSON.

Achievement pop-ups. Nine looks: Hybrid Launcher's own card, store-like, PlayStation-like and Xbox-like. Common achievements show as bronze, rare ones as silver or gold, and a game's platinum has its own. Place, size, time on screen, colours and sounds are yours to set.

Themes and colours. Colour themes such as the warm Cozy or Venom Violet (in the picture), your own accent, glow, background and icon colours, shading on or off and an interface size from 50 to 200%. The whole app follows, game pages included.

Auto tab (experimental). It reads the game and proposes the mods that fit, each with its build, then installs them together in the right order. Installing each mod from its own tab is still the recommended way.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Download the zip

    Get the latest HybridLauncher-…-win-x64.zip and extract it anywhere you can write, for example C:\Games\Hybrid Launcher.

  2. 2

    Run it

    Start HybridLauncher.exe. It finds the games of your launchers by itself, and a short tour shows you around.

  3. 3

    Pick a game, add a mod

    Open its page, choose OptiScaler or a mod in its tab, and press Install. Remove it any time and the folder goes back as it was; the Mods tab shows what is in the folder at any moment.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It is free and open source software under the GNU GPL v3: use it, read the code, build it, change it and fork it. Forks and unofficial builds just need to credit this project.

Does it change my game files?

Only the game folders you ask it to change, when you install a mod. The files a mod replaces can be backed up first (the default), so removing it puts the folder back as it was. Your settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HybridLauncher.

Do I need to sign in to my stores?

No. Without accounts it reads the games your launchers installed on this PC. Signing in to Steam, Epic or GOG only adds direct downloads and your full owned library.

Can mods get me banned?

They can, in games and services that forbid them. Check the rules of every game before you mod it, and never use mods in online or competitive games that do not allow them.

How does it update?

A few seconds after it starts it checks for a new version and asks once before updating. A new version also lands in the notification list beside Minimize, with a Windows pop-up you can turn off in Settings → Notifications.
